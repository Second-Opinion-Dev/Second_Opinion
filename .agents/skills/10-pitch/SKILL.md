# Stage 10: Pitch

Use `.agents/templates/PITCH.md`. Every number must come from a recorded result; state
limitations plainly (no ground truth, small data, untested assumptions). Lead with the decision
the water professional can now make, show the demo, then the method. Keep a backup
(screenshots or a recorded run) in case the live demo fails. Rehearse to time.
